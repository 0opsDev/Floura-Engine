## Floura Engine is a Open Source C++ Game Engine created for the purpose of learning OpenGL,

### Features:
---
#### Graphics:
~~~
. Forward Renderer (WIP)
. Deferred Renderer (WIP)
. RayTracer (WIP)
. Denoiser (WIP)
. TAA
. Gbuffer
. HBuffer
. Diffuse, Specular and Normals
. Point, Spot and Directional Lights (WIP)
. Cubemap Reflections (WIP)
. Shadow Mapping (WIP)

~~~
---
### Systems:
~~~
. Scenes
. ECS
. Collision Detection (WIP)
. Physics Dynamics
. UUIDs
. Lua Scripting via sol2
. 3D Directional Sound via openAL Soft (WIP)
~~~
---
### General Features:
~~~
. Assimp Model Loader
. Billboarding
~~~
---
### UI:
~~~
. ImGui Implementation
. Scene Hierarchy Window
. Scene Folder Window
. Inspector Tab
~~~
---
### Misc:
~~~
. Math Class
~~~

### Controls:
~~~
WASD: Move
Mouse: Look
Space: Fly Up/Jump
ctrl: Fly Down
Arrow UP/Down: Increase/Decrease Speed
Right Shift: Reset Speed
F1: Toggle Imgui
F2: Gravity and Collisions OFF
F3: Gravity and Collisions ON
~~~
